SOURCE TYPE: Publication

Submitted on 28 April 2026 by Siyuan Ji, this arXiv paper argues that AI tools are being deployed over MBSE models today, and those models were not designed for this kind of consumption — well-prompted frontier LLMs produce output over a conformant SysML model, but reasoning is drawn from training rather than retrieved from the model itself.

**SE Angle:** This is a textbook **interface allocation failure in a system-of-systems**. The BMD function was assumed to be satisfied at the joint force level by the Army — it was never formally written into USMC requirements.

 The insight: when a critical function is "covered" by an adjacent system with no formal interface requirement or capacity commitment, it becomes an emergent gap the moment operational context changes. No one owns the verification that the joint capability is sufficient — classic **verification gap** between system-level design intent and operational reality.
