**2. "Paving the Way for SysML v2: An ESA MBSE Methodology Implementation Review" (Duroy et al.)**
INCOSE *Systems Engineering* journal, published February 10, 2026. SOURCE TYPE: Publication
ESA developed an MBSE methodology originally based on SysML v1, applied across numerous space and non-space projects. With SysML v2's release, this paper pilots a migration: identifying v2 language changes, iteratively mapping ESA MBSE Methodology concepts to SysML v2 elements, and developing a textual notation language extension tested on a case study space mission.



Notably, the paper acknowledges there is "not yet a mature SysML v2 support" in current tool implementations, and it developed a SysML v2 language extension in textual notation applied to a reference mission model. A comparison of v1 and v2 graphical notation diagrams yielded key insights on the differences.
