## C2. IDDMBSE — Data-Driven + MBSE V-Process for Autonomous CPS

### Option 1 — Practitioner angle
A new paper extends the MBSE V-process for systems that keep learning after you ship them.

IDDMBSE adds a data-driven loop at every step of the classic V, anchored in SysML. The target is autonomous cyber-physical systems, where ML behaviour is part of the system, not a bolt-on. The authors released an open-source tool chain with it: PERFECT, TRADES-X, VERITAS.

Anyone working on autonomy already knows the awkward part. Your model captures the architecture, but the actual behaviour emerges from data you do not have at design time. IDDMBSE puts that data loop inside the process instead of leaving it to a separate ML team and a different toolset.

I would test this on a real subsystem before trusting the framing. But the gap it targets is real.

Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.06727

### Option 2 — Industry/trend angle (the adjacent threat)
The ML community is already building its own lifecycle discipline, and none of it speaks SysML.

IDDMBSE extends the MBSE V-process with a data-driven loop at every step, anchored in SysML, and ships with an open-source tool chain. It targets autonomous CPS, where behaviour comes from data, not just from architecture.

This matters because the ML side has MLOps, data versioning, and model governance moving fast. If MBSE stays document-and-diagram for the deterministic parts and waves at the learning parts, autonomy programmes will solve the problem without us and on their tools.

Work like this is the SE community trying to absorb the problem on our own terms. Watch the roadmap to re-formulate on SysML v2 and KerML, because that is where this either holds or fails.
