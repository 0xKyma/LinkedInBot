### Draft

I ran eight AI agents for a season of AFLW Fantasy. We finished 2,125th out of roughly 17,000. Here's what the season actually taught me about working with AI.

1. The goal shapes every decision. I told it to aim for 1st, not a top-500 finish, so it was built to take risks. A personal-best round still lost us 76 places because our team looked like everyone else's, and a late captain gamble cost 30 points. Be precise about what "winning" means.

2. "Unmeasurable" was the most expensive word. Three times we wrote a number down as unavailable without anyone checking. All three were public, and each mistake cost about a round.

3. More agents doesn't mean more coverage. Eight agents missed a free fix worth 34 points a round, for two rounds. A 200-line script found it. If a check can be plain arithmetic, write code.

4. Give something the power to say no. My red-team agent caught an illegal trade and numbers credited to the wrong player.

5. The boring automation paid off most. Scheduled checks before each match lockout, sent to my phone, caught every late team change.

2,125th isn't a win. But the AI was only as good as its goal, its data and the dull checks around it.
